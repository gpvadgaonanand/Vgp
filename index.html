About Gram Panchayat

Vadgaon Anand Gram Panchayat is a progressive local governing body in Junnar Taluka, Pune district. The Panchayat focuses on rural development, sanitation, digital governance, citizen welfare and infrastructure development.


The village is known for its cultural heritage, temples, public welfare schemes and active participation in social development. The Gram Panchayat continuously works towards building a smart and sustainable village.
